==============================================================================
5.  DIAGNOSTIC - THE 4-BIT EXPONENT HAS AN ABSOLUTE RANGE
==============================================================================
  SeedLM has no per-tensor or per-channel scale, and e is stored in
  4 bits, so e is confined to [-8, 7].  The method is therefore only
  scale invariant while the coefficients stay inside that window.
  Section 3.4 tunes (C,P,K) on standard-normal w (sigma=1), where the
  window is comfortable; real weight tensors sit far lower.

      sigma   rel.err      e range  % at floor  median cond(U) chosen
      1e+00    0.1228       [-4,0]        0.0%                    2.3
      1e-01    0.1226      [-7,-3]        0.0%                    2.4
      2e-02    0.1219      [-8,-6]       45.9%                    2.4
      1e-02    0.1288      [-8,-7]       97.5%                    2.4
      1e-03    0.4132       [-8,5]       72.5%                    6.6
      1e-04    0.5859       [-2,2]        0.0%               166630.0
      1e-05    0.5807      [-4,-1]        0.0%               170101.0

  Scale invariance would make every row identical; spread is 4.8x.
  Median cond(U) over all 65535 seeds is 2.6.  Once the
  exponent floor forces well-conditioned bases to quantise to zero, the
  search is left picking near-singular ones, whose huge coefficients are
  the only ones that survive - the method is outside its operating range.
